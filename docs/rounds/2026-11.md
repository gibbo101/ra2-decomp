## Round 226: local-count triage (17 November 2026)

locals ahead of its own temporaries, so some register and slot ties follow that count, by its
value mod 8 or by which run of eight it falls in. Names, places and scopes play no part, and
uninitialised locals do not count. tools/localprobe.py sweeps it. Eight Opus agents (high
effort) then swept 23 register-allocation near misses from +0 to +15 locals. Only the two
known cases follow the count. Do_MISSION_UNLOAD 0x0044D880 needs two more locals (or six
fewer), and only one has a sibling's backing. AI_Build_Defense 0x00506EF0 needs three to nine
more, none backed. The other 21 score the same at every count: they are genuine ties, not
local-count effects. Blit_Block's tint argument is an unsigned short: TechnoClass's
0x00706ED0 pushes it unmasked, as Bit_Blit's own declaration already has it. Gate: 19,139 of
19,139 functions, + 6 reconstructed.
